# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Briefed serves curious readers who want a calm, current overview of stories from sources they trust. They may arrive casually to scan headlines or return frequently to read a feed shaped by their chosen interests and publications.

## Product Purpose

Briefed makes RSS and Atom approachable through a public editorial homepage and lightweight personalization. Visitors receive value before registering, then choose interests and sources to create a focused newest-first timeline. Success means readers can understand the page immediately, find worthwhile stories quickly, and personalize without feeling blocked by account ceremony.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, calm, credible. Briefed should feel considered and current without behaving like a social network or an attention-maximizing news portal.

## Anti-references

- Social-media engagement dashboards with reactions, scores, follower counts, or noisy sharing controls.
- Boxed card grids that make every story look equally important.
- SaaS landing-page clichés, decorative gradients, glassmorphism, neon accents, and pink presentation backdrops.
- Forced onboarding that hides the public feed before a visitor understands the product.

## Design Principles

1. **Value before registration.** Let readers scan real stories before asking them to personalize.
2. **Editorial hierarchy over uniformity.** Story importance should be visible through scale, placement, and rhythm.
3. **Personalization should feel reversible.** Interests and sources are easy to choose, revise, dismiss, or cancel.
4. **Sources remain visible.** Every article clearly communicates where it came from.
5. **Keep the interface quiet.** Controls support reading and never compete with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All functionality must work with a keyboard, focus must remain visible, dialogs and drawers must manage focus correctly, color cannot be the only state indicator, reduced-motion preferences must be respected, and layouts must remain readable from small mobile screens through desktop widths.
